# Contributor conduct

Use respectful, constructive language. Review the work and evidence without attacking
the author. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and publishing credentials
or private reports are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation. Report
conduct problems privately to the repository maintainers through the repository's
available private contact channel. Do not post private identities or incident
details in a public issue. Security reports follow SECURITY.md.
